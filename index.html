HOW TO PLAY

To start the game, choose one of the three options: Rock, Paper, or Scissors by clicking on your preferred move. Once you make your selection, the computer will automatically choose its move at random. The game then compares both choices and decides the winner based on the classic rules of Rock Paper Scissors. If your move beats the computer’s move, you win the round; if the computer’s move beats yours, you lose; and if both choose the same option, the round ends in a draw. Each round updates the score, allowing you to track your performance over multiple games. You can continue playing as many rounds as you like or reset the game at any time to start fresh.
